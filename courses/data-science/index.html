Introduction to Data Science

One structured path from Python and statistics to machine learning models you can explain.

What Is Data Science?

Data science combines programming, statistics and machine learning to extract insights from data and predict future outcomes. Our Data Science training uses Python for Data Science from day one, so you learn by working with real datasets.

Data Science vs Data Analytics

Data analytics focuses on analysing existing data and reporting what happened and why. Data science goes further, building predictive models with machine learning to forecast what is likely to happen next.

Frequently Asked Questions About Data Science Courses

Who can learn data science? +

Anyone with an interest in numbers and problem-solving can start, including students, working professionals, and career switchers. A background in math, statistics, or programming helps, but isn't mandatory. Our course starts from the basics.

What topics does your data science course cover? +

Python programming, statistics and probability, data cleaning and visualization, machine learning, SQL, and real-world projects.

Do I need coding experience before joining? +

No. We begin with Python fundamentals before moving to advanced topics.

What career roles can I get after learning data science? +

Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Analyst, Business Analyst, and Research Associate are common roles.

Do you offer online and offline classes? +

We are offering only offline classroom training.

Will I receive a certificate? +

Yes, you'll receive a certificate of completion after successfully finishing the course and projects.

Do you provide placement assistance? +

We offer resume building, mock interviews, and job referrals. Please note that we assist with the process, but we cannot guarantee a job.

Are demo classes available? +

Yes, you can attend a free demo class before enrolling.
